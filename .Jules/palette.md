## 2025-05-14 - [Sticky Note & Placeholder Pattern]
**Learning:** Combining a visual metaphor (sticky note style) with an accessible CSS-only placeholder (`:empty::before`) and a subtle hover lift effect creates a delightful and intuitive 'visual editable' experience.
**Action:** Use `#fff9c4` for the sticky note background, `#fbc02d` for the accent border, and ensure placeholder contrast with a darker shade like `#7a754c`. Always apply `pointer-events: none` to the placeholder pseudo-element.
